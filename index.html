I'm a Research Engineer at Vijil, working on the safety, security and trust of AI agents, from formal guarantees to adversarial red-teaming.

Before that, I spent two years building open medical LLMs with the LiGHT lab (EPFL & Yale) and the MLO lab at EPFL, contributing to Meditron-70B and Llama-3-Meditron. I care about models that are useful where resources are scarce, which also took me to the International Committee of the Red Cross.

I hold an MSc in Data Science and a BSc in Computer Science from EPFL.
